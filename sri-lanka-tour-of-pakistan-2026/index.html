Sri Lanka Tour of Pakistan 2026

Sri Lanka's tour of Pakistan in October and November 2026 covers all three formats: a three-match T20I series (completed), an ODI tri-series involving Pakistan, Sri Lanka and England, and two Tests counting towards the World Test Championship. Follow every fixture here with live scores, the full schedule, and official highlights added after each game.

T20I Series — Results (Pakistan won 2-1)

  • 1st T20I, Oct 9 — Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium: Pakistan 187/6 beat Sri Lanka 182/8 by 4 wickets. Sahibzada Farhan scored 89.
  • 2nd T20I, Oct 11 — Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium: Pakistan 215/3 beat Sri Lanka 133/7 by 82 runs. Maaz Sadaqat scored 96 and Sahibzada Farhan 73.
  • 3rd T20I, Oct 13 — Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium: Sri Lanka 172/2 beat Pakistan 168/8 by 8 wickets — a consolation win for Sri Lanka. Kusal Mendis made 87 not out and Dasun Shanaka took 3/21.

ODI Tri-Series (Pakistan, Sri Lanka, England)

  • Oct 18 — Pakistan v Sri Lanka, Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium (1:00 PM PKT)
  • Oct 20 — Pakistan v England, Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium
  • Oct 22 — Sri Lanka v England, Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium
  • Oct 25 — Pakistan v England, Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore
  • Oct 27 — Pakistan v Sri Lanka, Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore
  • Oct 29 — Sri Lanka v England, Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore
  • Oct 31 — Final, Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore

Tests (World Test Championship)

  • Nov 9–13 — 1st Test, Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium
  • Nov 17–21 — 2nd Test, Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore
  • Nov 4–6 — 3-day warm-up, Islamabad
